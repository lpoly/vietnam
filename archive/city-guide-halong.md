# Northern → Central Vietnam with Ha Long Bay: the guide

Nine nights, six places, one line south: Hanoi's lakes and monuments, a night on Ha Long Bay, the limestone karst of Ninh Binh, a night on the train, the caves of Phong Nha, the imperial city and tombs of Hue, and over the Hai Van Pass into Hoi An. Nature and the must-see landmarks come first; the food is what happens in between, and it's very good.

Every price, time and address below comes from the full itinerary, re-checked in October 2026. Where sources still disagree, it says so here too, and those bits are flagged. The hour-by-hour schedule lives in the Plan tab.

A few things worth knowing before anything else: 12 December 2026 is a **Saturday**, so Hanoi is a weekend, the bay is Monday night, Ninh Binh is Tuesday–Wednesday (fewer coaches), and Hue is Friday–Saturday (busier). Prices use 1 USD ≈ 26,000 VND. And **carry cash**: street food, markets, most homestays, park gates and boats don't take cards.

## Hanoi

*12–13 December · 2 nights · Days 1–2*

The capital, and the place to see the landmarks of modern Vietnam in two days: Hoan Kiem Lake and its island temple, the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum, the 1070 Temple of Literature, the Hoa Lo Prison. You're based in the Old Quarter, roughly 1 km across, where the street names are still the trades that once lined them.

You're here on the right weekend: the roads around Hoan Kiem Lake close to traffic from Friday evening to Sunday midnight.

### At a glance

- **When** — Saturday 12 and Sunday 13 December. The mausoleum is closed Mondays and Fridays, so Sunday morning is the slot.
- **Weather** — 15–22°C with lows near 14°C, humid and drizzly. Buildings are not heated.
- **Don't miss** — The mausoleum at 08:00 on Sunday, the Temple of Literature, the lake at night.
- **Signature dish** — *Bún chả*, the dish that explains northern cooking in one bowl.
- **Sleep** — Old Quarter, two minutes from the lake.

### See and do

- **Hoan Kiem Lake and Ngoc Son Temple** — The centre of the city. The temple sits on an island in the lake, reached by the red bridge: 30,000 VND, roughly 07:00–18:00. Start here on the first afternoon.
- **Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum** — Free, open Sundays 08:00–11:00 in the cool season, closed Mondays and Fridays. The queue is long and the rules are strict: shoulders and knees covered, no shorts, bags stored, phones and cameras collected, silence. Arrive early.
- **The Presidential Palace grounds and the stilt house** — Behind the mausoleum, 25,000 or 40,000 VND, open mornings. The stilt house where Ho Chi Minh lived is the quiet, interesting part. The **One Pillar Pagoda** next door is free.
- **Temple of Literature** — 58 Quoc Tu Giam St; 08:00–17:00 (last entry 16:30); 70,000 VND, cash. Vietnam's 1070 Confucian academy; the stelae of doctoral laureates sit on stone tortoises in the third courtyard. An optional **night tour** with a 3D mapping show runs typically Thu–Sun, 199,000 VND.
- **Hoa Lo Prison** — 08:00–17:00 daily, ~50,000 VND (sources range 30,000–50,000). The French colonial prison later known as the "Hanoi Hilton". About an hour, ten minutes' walk south of the lake.
- **Museum of Ethnology** — In Cau Giay, 08:30–17:30 (closed Mondays), 40,000 VND, 25 minutes by Grab. The outdoor collection of reconstructed minority houses is the best thing of its kind in the country.
- **Thang Long Imperial Citadel** — 100,000 VND, 08:00–17:00. The excavated royal citadel near the mausoleum: the alternative if the group would rather walk than do another museum.
- **Thang Long Water Puppet Theatre** — 57B Dinh Tien Hoang, on the lake. 100,000–200,000 VND by seat. Shows at 16:10, 17:20, 18:30 and 20:00; book 3–7 days ahead, direct with the theatre.
- **Hoan Kiem weekend walking street** — Friday 19:00 to Sunday midnight, suspended in bad weather. Families, badminton, street games: Hanoi's public life at its least filtered. Free.
- **Long Bien Bridge** — The steel cantilever bridge over the Red River whose builder's plate reads 1899–1902, Daydé & Pillé, Paris. Walk the upstream side at dusk, when it fills with commuters, couples and people fishing off the piers. Stand aside for scooters.

### Eat and drink

Northern food is restrained, savoury, sour and herb-forward, with dill in places you won't see it elsewhere in Vietnam.

- **Bún Chả Đắc Kim** — 1 Hang Manh St, Hoan Kiem, ~10:00–14:00, 70,000–100,000 VND/person. **Bún chả** and the square sea-crab spring rolls, **nem cua bể**. In the 2026 Michelin Guide, still a plastic-stool operation. Five minutes from the lake: the first lunch.
- **Chả Cá Thăng Long** — 6B Duong Thanh St, Cua Dong Ward, Hoan Kiem. ~200,000–250,000 VND/person. One dish only: turmeric catfish finished at your table with a hedge of dill, over vermicelli with peanuts and shrimp paste. **Reserve for eight.**
- **Phở Hà** — 15 Hang Hom St, Hang Gai, Hoan Kiem. ~60,000–80,000 VND. **Phở gà** and **bún thang**, the most technically demanding dish in the Hanoi repertoire.
- **Cafe Dinh** — 13 Dinh Tien Hoang, up an unmarked staircase beside a silk shop, overlooking the lake. Egg coffee, ~30,000 VND.
- **Bia hơi** — Fresh draught beer, 15,000–20,000 VND a glass, at the corner of **Nguyen Huu Huan and Hang Thung** or **Bia Hơi Hải Xồm**, rather than backpacker Ta Hien.

### Markets and shops

- **Hang Bac** — The silversmiths' street, on the Old Quarter loop: Ma May → Hang Bac → **Hang Ma** (paper goods) → **Dong Xuan Market** → Hang Chieu → Hang Buom.
- **Chợ Châu Long (Chau Long Market)** — Chau Long St, Ba Dinh, from around 05:30, busiest 06:00–08:00. A working neighbourhood market, ten minutes' walk from the mausoleum: breakfast of **bánh cuốn** and **xôi xéo** before the queue. Small notes only.

### Where we sleep

- **La Siesta Premium Hang Be** — 27 Hang Be, two minutes from the lake. Rated 9.5; weekend average ~$83 a room in October 2026.
- **Hanoi La Siesta Classic Ma May** — 94 Ma May St. Rated 9.5; pricier at weekends (~$152 a room in the October snapshot). Rooftop bar with a view to Long Bien Bridge.
- **Hanoi Emerald Waters Hotel & Spa** — 47 Lo Su. Rated 9.5; ~$88 a room.

### Getting around

- **From the airport** — A pre-booked 16-seat van from Noi Bai Terminal 2, ~1 hour, ~1,200,000 VND. Don't rely on airport taxi touts with eight people and eight bags.
- **Grab** — Download it before you fly and add a card. A Grab bike across the Old Quarter is 20,000–30,000 VND.
- **To the bay** — At 08:00 on Monday, by the cruise's shuttle or a private 16-seat van: 2.5–3 hours to Tuan Chau harbour.
- **Other ways from the airport** — Two 6-seat Grab cars from T2 car park P1 or P7 (about 250,000 VND for a 4-seater to the centre in April 2026). Or two metered taxis from the official rank, 280,000–400,000 VND each (sources disagree). Bus 86 runs to the Old Quarter's edge for 45,000–50,000 VND each, every 25–30 minutes. Sources: [Noi Bai Airport](https://www.noibaiairport.vn/en/public-transportations-nid1.html), [Grab, Aug 2026](https://www.grab.com/vn/en/blog/cap-nhat-diem-don-va-gia-cuoc-grabcar-tai-san-bay-noi-bai/), [Eternal Arrival, Apr 2026](https://eternalarrival.com/grab-at-hanoi-airport-noi-bai/).

### Good to know

- **Dress for the mausoleum** — Shoulders and knees covered on Sunday morning; the same goes for the pagodas and the Hue tombs later.
- **Train Street** — Groups are banned and barriers are up; the cafés operate in a grey zone under police watch. Skip it.
- **Crossing the road** — Cross at a constant speed without stopping. Scooters flow around you.

## Ha Long Bay

*14–15 December · 1 night, on the boat · Days 3–4*

Vietnam's best-known landscape: limestone islands standing in the sea, seen by kayak in the afternoon and from the deck at sunset. You spend one night on a boat that boards at **Tuan Chau**, 2.5–3 hours from Hanoi, and the next morning you go straight on to Ninh Binh without going back through the capital. The night comes out of Phong Nha, which drops to one.

There are really three bays here. **Ha Long Bay** proper has the famous caves and viewpoints and the most boats. **Lan Ha Bay**, its southern neighbour, is quieter, with the best kayaking and lagoons. **Bai Tu Long**, to the north-east, is the quietest. Since April 2026 overnight boats can sail from Ha Long into Lan Ha, and most Lan Ha boats now leave from Tuan Chau too. For December, a Lan Ha boat is the pick.

### At a glance

- **When** — Monday 14 December, boarding around noon, back on land by 11:00 on Tuesday.
- **Weather** — Days around 18–22°C, evenings near 15°C, sea 21–23°C. Mornings are often misty, usually clearing by midday.
- **Don't miss** — Kayaking among the karst, and sunset from the deck.
- **Sleep** — On the boat: four cabins together.

### See and do

- **Kayaking or a bamboo boat** — Among the islands, only in designated zones, so always with the boat's guide. Lan Ha's lagoons are the best of it.
- **Dark & Bright Cave** — On the Lan Ha route.
- **Sung Sot cave** — On the Ha Long route: the bay's big show cave.
- **Ti Top island** — On the Ha Long route: a climb to a viewpoint over the bay.
- **Viet Hai** — A village on Cat Ba island where some Lan Ha boats stop. If yours offers it, take it over a second cave.
- **Swimming** — Possible, but in December it's a short dip around midday, not the point.

### Where we sleep

- **Indochine Cruise** — Lan Ha route, from Tuan Chau. From US$150 per person.
- **Heritage Binh Chuan** — Lan Ha, 20 suites. From US$180 per person, with meals, kayaking, caves and tickets; transfers and drinks extra.
- **Indochine Premium** — Ha Long Bay proper, 37 cabins. From US$175 per person, including the caves, Ti Top, meals and fees.

### Getting around

- **From Hanoi** — The cruise's shuttle (about US$30–40 per person for the round trip) or a private 16-seat van, 2,700,000 VND on one rate sheet: 2.5–3 hours to Tuan Chau. The seaplane from Noi Bai stopped on 1 April 2026. Sources: [Local Vietnam, Jul 2026](https://localvietnam.com/routes/from-hanoi-to-halong-bay/), [Indochina Tour, May 2026](https://www.indochinatour.com/vietnam-guide/halong-bay-cruise-price.html).
- **On to Ninh Binh** — A private 16-seat van from the pier at about 11:00, 3–3.5 hours via Hai Phong; Vivu Halong lists one at US$120. Or the Cat Ba Express shared limousine at 11:40, from US$15, one medium suitcase each. Sources: [Vivu Halong](https://vivuhalong.com/en/transfer/halong-to-ninh-binh-private-car-transfer/), [Cat Ba Express](https://catbaexpress.com/limousine-bus-halong-to-ninh-binh.html).

### Good to know

- **Fog and wind can stop the boats** — From December to February the authorities sometimes suspend departures, deciding on the day or the afternoon before. Complete cancellations are rare. If it's cancelled before you leave Hanoi, go straight to Tam Coc a day early and book a second night there. Source: [Bhaya](https://bhayacruises.com/blog/halong-bay-on-storm/).
- **Bring everything** — You check out of Hanoi with all your bags. Ask the boat to store the suitcases; no source says how much the cabins take.
- **Prices are "from" prices** — No December 2026 quote was found for any boat. The bay fees are usually included.

## Ninh Binh

*15–16 December · 1 night · Days 4–5*

Limestone, rice paddies and rowed boat routes through flooded caves: the best karst-and-river landscape reachable in the dry northern winter. You stay around **Tam Coc**, a 20-minute drive from Ninh Binh city, and you're here on a Tuesday and Wednesday, which means fewer domestic coaches.

It's one night and two full days, so the timing matters: Van Long on the afternoon you arrive from the bay, Trang An at 07:30 the next morning before the coaches, Mua Cave and Bích Động in the afternoon, then the night train south.

### At a glance

- **When** — Tuesday 15 and Wednesday 16 December; the train leaves Wednesday night.
- **Weather** — 15–21°C.
- **Don't miss** — Trang An by rowed boat at 07:30, the Mua Cave view, Van Long at dusk.
- **Signature dish** — *Dê núi*, mountain goat, and *cơm cháy* crisped rice.
- **Sleep** — Tam Coc, with two rooms kept until the train.

### See and do

- **Trang An, Route 2** — A UNESCO mixed site: 2.5–3 hours by rowed boat through flooded caves, some low enough that you duck, past temples and the *Kong: Skull Island* set. **300,000 VND/person** since 1 January 2026, open 07:00–16:30. Boats hold 4–5. Arrive by 07:30: the coaches land between 09:00 and 11:00.
- **Mua Cave (Hang Múa)** — **486 stone steps** to a Quan Am statue and a dragon ridge with the best aerial view of the Tam Coc valley in the province. **150,000 VND** since April 2026, open 06:00–19:00. Hard work in the last third; there's a café and lotus pond at the bottom for anyone who'd rather not. Skip it if it's wet.
- **Van Long Nature Reserve** — A wetland with the same karst but a fraction of the traffic, 40 minutes north. Flat-bottomed boats rowed by local women, 1h30 under limestone cliffs. Around 20,000 VND entry plus a 50,000–60,000 VND boat (one list says 100,000 all-in), **cash only**. Go late afternoon, when the herons come in to roost and, if you're lucky, the Delacour's langurs come down the cliffs.
- **Bích Động Pagoda** — Free, 2 km from Tam Coc wharf. Three tiers of temple built into a limestone cliff, reached by a stone stair through a cave. Usually nearly empty.
- **Hoa Lu ancient capital** — 20,000 VND. Vietnam's capital from 968 to 1010: two modest temples to the Dinh and Le emperors in a bowl of limestone. Forty minutes, history rather than spectacle.
- **Bái Đính Pagoda** — Free, electric cart 60,000 VND. The largest pagoda complex in Vietnam, vast and covered: the wet-weather alternative to Mua Cave.
- **Phát Diệm Cathedral** — Free, ~30 km south. An 1891 stone cathedral built in Vietnamese temple form. The rain-day detour.

### Eat and drink

- **Goat** — **Nhà hàng Đức Dê** or any of the goat restaurants signposted *dê núi* along the Tam Coc road, ~200,000 VND/person: **dê tái chanh** (goat "cooked" in lime), **dê nướng lá lốt** (grilled in betel leaf) and **cơm cháy**. *Hours for individual goat restaurants can't be verified.*
- **Dinner at the hotel** — Ask for **ốc nấu chuối đậu**, snails stewed with green banana and tofu, a northern home dish you'll almost never see on a menu.
- **Before the train** — There's only instant noodles and beer on board: eat properly, and buy water, fruit and *bánh mì* from the shops on the approach to the station.

### Where we sleep

- **Tam Coc River View Homestay** — 300 m from Tam Coc wharf; five rooms, so the eight of you can take the whole property.
- **Tam Coc Rice Fields Resort** — From about €39 a room.
- **Tam Coc Garden** — Now a boutique resort, from about US$248 a night for two.

### Getting around

- **From the bay** — 190–215 km from Tuan Chau via Hai Phong, 3–3.5 hours by 16-seat van; going back through Hanoi takes 5–6.
- **Other ways from the bay** — The Long Travel runs Ha Long → Ninh Binh hourly 06:00–18:00 for 460,000 VND, and Daytrip sells seats from €33 per person, one suitcase each. Sources: [Baolau, Ha Long → Ninh Binh](https://www.baolau.com/s/ha-long/ninh-binh), [Daytrip](https://daytrip.com/en/transfers/ha-long-vn/tam-coc-vn).
- **Bicycles** — From the hotel, free or ~50,000 VND a day: ride the lanes between Tam Coc and Ninh Hai through farmyards and paddies.

### Good to know

- **Keep two rooms until 21:00** — The train leaves around 22:00; nobody publishes day-use rates, so book a second night for two rooms or get late check-out in writing.
- **Boats split the group** — Trang An boats hold 4–5, Van Long boats 2–3. Agree who's with whom and where to meet.

## The night train

*16–17 December · 1 night, on rails · Day 5*

The biggest time win of the trip: **Train SE19** turns a 9-hour road slog into a night you'd have spent in bed anyway, from Ninh Binh at 21:57 to Dong Hoi at 05:16. *Times still disagree between sources (departure 21:51–22:21, arrival 04:40–06:00); reconfirm on dsvn.vn.*

### Where we sleep

- **Two 4-berth soft-sleeper cabins** — Bedding, air-con and a power socket per berth. Pack an overnight bag so you're not opening suitcases at 05:00.

### Getting around

- **Ninh Binh Railway Station** — 1 Hoang Dieu St (some listings say 41 Hoang Hoa Tham; the station has been re-addressed). Swap the e-ticket voucher for a paper ticket 30–60 minutes before departure.
- **Dong Hoi station** — Thuan Ly St (now Quang Tri province, formerly Quang Binh). Two 7-seat transfers take you the 45 km to Phong Nha by about 06:30.
- **Other trains and buses** — SE3 leaves Ninh Binh about 21:36 and reaches Dong Hoi about 04:45; SE1 leaves around midnight and arrives 06:30–07:21 (listings disagree). Sleeper and cabin buses go from Tam Coc straight to Phong Nha in the evening, 6–9 hours, 650,000 VND with HK Bus Lines or €13–22 with The Long Travel, Camel or Full Moon Party, but most arrive in the middle of the night. Sources: [Baolau trains](https://www.baolau.com/en/s/Ninh-Binh/Dong-Hoi/train), [Baolau buses](https://www.baolau.com/en/s/Ninh-Binh/Phong-Nha), [Gecko Routes](https://geckoroutes.com/vietnam/ninh-binh-to-phong-nha/).

## Phong Nha

*17–18 December · 1 night · Days 6–7*

The nature centrepiece: world-class karst, real caving, and the least performative tourism economy on the route. The base is **Son Trach**, Phong Nha town, now in **Quang Tri province**.

One cave day in this version, because the bay took a night: the show caves (Phong Nha Cave by boat, Paradise Cave in the afternoon), then an early scooter loop through the Bong Lai valley before the drive south. A guided Oxalis trek into the wild caves can replace the show caves, not join them.

### At a glance

- **When** — Thursday 17 to Friday 18 December; you arrive off the train at about 06:30.
- **Weather** — Caves are caves; rain only matters for the road to Paradise Cave.
- **Don't miss** — Paradise Cave's first kilometre, Bong Lai at 07:00.
- **Signature dish** — *Cá kho tộ*, fish braised in a clay pot.
- **Sleep** — Phong Nha Farmstay in the paddies, or a family homestay in town.

### See and do

- **Paradise Cave (Thiên Đường)** — A boardwalk through a chamber so large the lighting doesn't reach the ceiling; the full cave runs 31 km. **270,000 VND** including the buggy (older sources say 250,000 plus the buggy). **It closes at 16:00**: arrive by 13:30.
- **Phong Nha Cave by boat** — You motor 1.5 km up an underground river into the cave, then walk out onto a sandbar. **150,000 VND/person**, plus a boat priced per boat (max 12). *Sources give 550,000 VND, 750,000 VND or 55,000 VND per person for the boat.* **Tien Son Cave** above it is 80,000 VND.
- **Hang Tien Cave Day Trek** — Instead of the show caves, if the group would rather trek. The Oxalis day for a mixed group in December: a dry cave with an enormous entrance and huge calcite formations, and a pool you can decline. 2,000,000 VND / US$75, max 12, ages 16–70; back around 19:30–20:00.
- **Oxalis Tu Lan Experience (1 day)** — The wetter alternative: 9 km of valleys and river crossings, a **15 m ladder descent on harness and rope**, and **150 m swimming through an underground river**. Same price. December cave water is 20–22°C with no sun afterward. *Oxalis's pages disagree on its season.*
- **Bong Lai valley** — A farming basin 10 km out of Son Trach: 25–30 km of quiet farm road by scooter (~150,000–200,000 VND each), past the **Duck Stop**, **Wild Boar Eco Farm** and **the Pub With Cold Beer**. It's become a circuit with mid-morning minibuses; **ride it at 07:00**.
- **The Phong Nha Caves Exploration Museum** — Free, 08:00–17:30. Where you learn how Hồ Khanh, a local logger, found Son Doong's entrance in 1991 while sheltering from a storm.
- **Dark Cave** — The rain backup: zipline, mud bath and kayak, *350,000 or 450,000 VND*. Book in a Phong Nha town office, not through Hanoi or Hoi An agencies.

### Eat and drink

River fish, eel, chicken and clay-pot braises: the plainest cooking on the route, and good.

- **Chợ Troc / Son Trach morning market** — From ~05:30, 30,000–40,000 VND/person. **Bánh mì** and a bowl of **bún** straight off the train.
- **Phong Nha Bamboo Restaurant** or **Mountain View Restaurant** — On the main street, 80,000–150,000 VND/person: **cá kho tộ** and **lươn xào nghệ** (turmeric eel). *Turnover is high; ask your hosts what's good this month.*

### Where we sleep

- **Phong Nha Farmstay** — 8 km out of town in the paddies. Standard rooms 1,300,000 VND, family rooms 2,400,000 VND (direct, October 2026). Pool, free bikes, good food. It may want two nights; ask, or take a homestay.
- **Thao Nguyen Homestay** or **Jungle Boss Homestay** — In town, family-run, roughly US$15–40 a room. **Several Phong Nha homestays are cash-only.**

### Getting around

- **From the train** — Oxalis transfers, 650,000 VND per 7-seater, ~1 hour. Ask for early check-in: you arrive about 06:30.
- **Other ways from the train** — Taxis wait outside Dong Hoi station, 400,000–600,000 VND per car (sources disagree), though no one confirms they're there at 05:00. A single 8-passenger minivan is €48 on Bookaway, and most Phong Nha hotels do station pickups. Sources: [Gecko Routes](https://www.geckoroutes.com/vietnam/dong-hoi-to-phong-nha/), [Phong Nha Farmstay](https://phongnhafarmstay.com/to-phong-nha/).
- **To Hue** — A van via the **Vinh Moc Tunnels**, ~5 hours with the stop, 3,000,000–3,380,000 VND plus US$15–20 for the detour.
- **Other ways to Hue** — The new Hue–Phong Nha tourist train, HQ1, leaves Tho Loc (about 20 km away) daily at 13:15 and reaches Hue at 17:25, 210,000–760,000 VND depending on the seat (sources disagree). TP Travel's buses leave Phong Nha at 09:00 and 11:30, about 4 hours, 350,000 VND. Neither stops at Vinh Moc. Sources: [Oxalis, tourist train](https://oxalisadventure.com/hue-phong-nha-tourist-train/), [Baolau, Phong Nha → Hue](https://www.baolau.com/s/Phong-Nha/Hue).

### Good to know

- **Cash** — ATMs in Son Trach are sparse to nonexistent. Withdraw in Hanoi or Ninh Binh city.
- **Hospital** — The nearest serious hospital is in Dong Hoi, 45 km away.
- **Socks** — Calf-length and thick, for leeches on wet trails.

## Hue

*18–19 December · 2 nights · Days 7–8*

The imperial capital of the Nguyen dynasty, with two nights now so you can see both halves of it: the walled Imperial City in the morning, and the emperors' tombs in the hills along the Perfume River in the afternoon. It's also Vietnam's most distinctive regional cooking: court dishes in miniature, and chilli-and-shrimp-paste noodle soups.

On the way in from Phong Nha you cross the old DMZ: the Vinh Moc Tunnels and the bridge at the 17th parallel.

### At a glance

- **When** — Friday 18 (arrive 15:30) and Saturday 19 December.
- **Weather** — A high of about 24°C and a low of 19°C, and around 350 mm of rain in December. Bring a shell.
- **Don't miss** — The Imperial City at opening, the tombs of Tu Duc, Khai Dinh and Minh Mang, Thien Mu at sunset.
- **Signature dish** — *Bún bò Huế*, for breakfast.
- **Sleep** — The south bank, a 10-minute walk from Dong Ba Market.

### See and do

- **Hue Imperial City (Đại Nội)** — The walled royal city: Thai Hoa Palace, the Nine Dynastic Urns, the To Mieu ancestral temple and the restored Kien Trung Palace. **200,000 VND** alone, or **530,000 VND** for the combo with three tombs, valid two days. *Opening is 07:00 in the most recent source, 06:30 in older ones.* Allow 2–3 hours.
- **Tomb of Tu Duc** — About 7 km out: pavilions and a lake in a wooded park, the emperor's retreat as much as his tomb. About an hour; **Tu Hieu Pagoda** is close by.
- **Tomb of Khai Dinh** — About 10 km out: the steep, dark concrete-and-mosaic one, unlike the others. 40–45 minutes, with a long stair.
- **Tomb of Minh Mang** — About 12 km out: the most symmetrical of the three, an axis of courtyards and lakes. About 50 minutes.
- **Thien Mu Pagoda** — Free, 08:00–18:00, ~4 km upriver of the Citadel. The seven-storey tower on the river bank, Hue's emblem. Come back to town by **dragon boat** at sunset (*~250,000 to 400,000+ VND; agree before boarding*).
- **Vinh Moc Tunnels** — On the drive from Phong Nha: a village of 300 people lived underground here for six years, with a maternity room where 17 children were born. Low, dark and narrow; the most affecting hour on the route. *50,000 or 100,000 VND.* **Hien Luong Bridge** at the 17th parallel is ten minutes on.
- **Truong Tien Bridge** — Walk the riverfront on Le Loi and cross to the Dong Ba side on Friday evening.

### Eat and drink

- **Bún bò Huế** — Breakfast: **Bún Bò Huế Bà Tuyết**, 47 Nguyen Cong Tru St, ~50,000 VND, or **Quán Cẩm**, 38 Tran Cao Van, roughly 06:00–19:00. *The best shops close when the pot empties.*
- **Hue rice cakes** — **Quán Bà Đỏ**, 7 Nguyen Binh Khiem, or **Hàng Me Mé**, 60 Tran Quang Khai: **bánh bèo**, **bánh bột lọc**, **bánh nậm** and **bánh ram ít** as a set, 120,000–180,000 VND/person. **Reserve for eight.**
- **Bánh khoái** — **Lạc Thiện**, 6 Dinh Tien Hoang, or **Chị Hạnh**, 11–15 Pho Duc Chinh, ~100,000 VND: a crisp turmeric pancake with **nem lụi** and peanut-and-liver sauce. **Reserve for eight at Lạc Thiện.**
- **Cơm hến** — **Cơm Hến Bà Đen** (06:00–11:30) or **Cơm hến Hương**, ~30,000 VND: cold rice with baby clams from the Perfume River and a lot of chilli. Sunday's breakfast before the bikes.

### Markets and shops

- **Chợ Đông Ba (Dong Ba Market)** — By Truong Tien Bridge, roughly 06:00–19:00. Hue's central market; in the evening look for *bánh ép*, rice-and-shrimp batter pressed on a hot iron, 10,000 VND a piece.

### Where we sleep

- **White Lotus Hue** — 5–7 Hoang Hoa Tham, south bank. Rated 9.2; ~US$68 a room with breakfast.
- **Alba Spa Hotel** — 29 Tran Quang Khai, Phu Hoi. ~US$28–30 a room; a 10-minute walk to Dong Ba.
- **Silk Path Grand Hue** — 2 Le Loi, on the river. ~US$59–110 a room.

### Getting around

- **The tomb day** — A 16-seat van with driver: **Hue Private Cars** lists US$50 for four sites, US$65 for five; entry tickets not included.
- **On foot** — From the south-bank hotels it's a 10-minute walk to Dong Ba across Truong Tien Bridge.

### Good to know

- **Rain** — In heavy rain keep the Imperial City and Khai Dinh, the most covered tomb, and add the Hue Royal Antiquities Museum (50,000 VND).
- **Book early** — Hue on a Friday and Saturday night is a weekend in a compact hotel district.

## Hai Van Pass and Hoi An

*20 December · 1 night · Day 9*

The last day is the ride: about 150 km from Hue over the **Hai Van Pass**, 21 km of switchbacks topping out around 500 m, with Lang Co lagoon on one side and Da Nang Bay on the other, then down the coast past the **Marble Mountains** into **Hoi An**. Motorbikes are banned from the Hai Van tunnel, which keeps the pass quiet: the trucks are underneath you.

Hoi An's UNESCO old town is the reward: lanterns, the river, assembly halls and the Japanese Covered Bridge, and *cao lầu*, a noodle dish that only exists here. The airport is 40–50 minutes away for the flight home.

### At a glance

- **When** — Sunday 20 December; fly from Da Nang on Monday morning.
- **Weather** — Wet season tail; Hoi An's flood risk runs to mid-December. Watch the forecast.
- **Don't miss** — The view from the top of Hai Van, the Japanese Covered Bridge, a lantern boat on the Thu Bon.
- **Signature dish** — *Cao lầu*, Hoi An only.
- **Sleep** — Walking distance of the old town.

### See and do

- **Hai Van Pass** — Stop at the top: French bunkers, a Nguyen-dynasty gate, and the view back over Lang Co and ahead to Da Nang Bay. Go slowly on the descent: tight switchbacks, patched surface, sea mist on the south side.
- **Lang Co / Lap An Lagoon** — Oyster rafts and mountains behind; the fuel stop and seafood lunch at the foot of the climb.
- **Marble Mountains** — Limestone hills with caves and pagodas inside, on the coast road south of Da Nang. *40,000 VND to climb or 80,000 with the lift; the listing is ambiguous.*
- **Hoi An Ancient Town** — A ticket for foreigners is **120,000 VND** and covers a set of the old houses, assembly halls, museums and the **Japanese Covered Bridge** (reopened in August 2024). The sites close at 17:00, so go straight to one or two; after that the old town is the streets themselves, pedestrian until 21:00 in winter. *Sources describe what the ticket covers differently.*
- **Lantern boat on the Thu Bon** — About 170,000 VND for 1–3 people, 220,000 for 4–5, roughly 16:00–21:30. The **night market** on Nguyen Hoang runs from about 17:00.
- **The Heritage Train, Hue → Da Nang** — The rain version of the day: **HĐ1** 07:05 → 10:10, **HĐ3** 14:30 → 18:15, 180,000–500,000 VND, with a stop at **Lang Co station**. *Sunday running in December isn't confirmed.*

### Eat and drink

- **Seafood at Lang Co** — Grilled squid, clams and oysters at a beach *quán*, 150,000–250,000 VND/person. Don't eat heavily before the descent.
- **Mì Quảng Bà Mua** — If you stop in Da Nang: several branches (95A Nguyen Tri Phuong and 19 Tran Binh Trong are the most named), **35,000–50,000 VND**. *Some branches may be defunct.*
- **Cao lầu** — Thick, chewy noodles with char siu, greens and crackling, made with water from a specific Hoi An well. Order it with *mì Quảng* at a busy local place in the old town.

### Where we sleep

- **Emm Hotel Hoi An** — 0.7 km from the old town, rated 9.2, from ~US$76 a room.
- **River Suites Hoi An** — 0.6 km, rated 8.8, from ~US$66.
- **Hoi An Historic Hotel** — 0.5 km, rated 9.1, from ~US$99.

### Getting around

- **The bikes** — One-way Hue → Hoi An with luggage transfer: **Hue Scooter Rental** (from US$25 per bike, drop-off at 200 Le Thanh Tong), **Motorvina** (from US$36 including bag transfer) or **Style Motorbikes**. Take **semi-automatic or manual 110–150 cc** for engine braking on the descent.
- **Other ways from Hue** — For anyone who won't ride, or if it rains: a 12-seat van over the pass with the same stops, US$99 from Hue Private Cars, 5–7 hours. Or the Heritage Train to Da Nang and a 300,000–400,000 VND taxi on to Hoi An. Limousine buses take about 3 hours for 199,000–320,000 VND, but through the tunnel, so you miss the views. Sources: [Hue Private Cars](https://hueprivatecars.com/tour/hue-hoi-an-1-day/), [Good Morning Hoi An, Apr 2026](https://goodmorning-hoian.com/en/hue-to-hoi-an/).
- **To the airport** — 40–50 minutes, up to 90 in heavy rain. Grab is scarce at night: book a car or van the evening before, 450,000–750,000 VND.
- **Other ways to the airport** — Grab can be booked up to 90 days ahead, about 350,000–450,000 VND per 7-seater, but a driver isn't guaranteed. Taxis (Mai Linh 0236 356 5656, Vinasun 0236 368 6868) run 24 hours for 300,000–450,000 VND. Barri Ann's shared shuttle leaves at 06:00 and takes about 90 minutes. Sources: [Grab advance booking](https://www.grab.com/vn/transport/advance-booking/), [Da Nang Airport](https://danangairport.vn/airport-guide-arrival-guide), [Barri Ann Travel](https://barrianntravel.com/st_activity/shared-shuttle-bus-from-hoi-an-to-da-nang/).

### Good to know

- **Motorbike licences** — Vietnam recognises only the **1968** Convention International Driving Permit, showing category A, carried with your original licence. Riding without one voids your travel and medical insurance. Anyone on a 1949 IDP takes the van or the train.
- **If it rains, don't ride** — A wet Hai Van Pass with eight riders on unfamiliar bikes is the highest-risk item of the trip. Take the train or a van; decide by 07:00.
- **If Hoi An floods** — Risk runs to mid-December. Sleep in Da Nang's Hai Chau instead, 15 minutes from the airport.
