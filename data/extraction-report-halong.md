## Food entries

- D1 lunch 12:45 [Hanoi] **Bún Chả Đắc Kim** — Bún Chả Đắc Kim
    - hours: Open ~10:00–14:00.
    - price: 70,000–100,000 VND/person.
    - order: Order **bún chả** (grilled pork patties and belly in a sweet-sour fish sauce broth with a mountain of herbs) and **nem cua bể**, the square sea-crab spring rolls.
    - reason: It's the best first meal in Vietnam because it explains the whole northern flavour logic in one bowl, and it's five minutes from the lake.
- D1 dinner 19:30 [Hanoi] **Chả Cá Thăng Long** — Chả Cá Thăng Long
    - price: ~200,000–250,000 VND/person.
    - reserve: **Reserve for eight**: call the day before; the pale-yellow townhouse at 6B is the good branch of three on the same street.
    - reason: One dish only: **chả cá**, turmeric-marinated catfish grilled in banana leaf, then finished at your table with a hedge of dill and spring onion, over rice vermicelli with peanuts and *mắm tôm* shrimp paste.
- D1 other 16:30 [Hanoi] **Coffee at **Cafe Dinh**, 13 Dinh Tien Hoang** — Cafe Dinh
    - price: Egg coffee (*cà phê trứng*), ~30,000 VND.
    - reason: Coffee at **Cafe Dinh**, 13 Dinh Tien Hoang — up an unmarked staircase beside a silk shop, second floor, overlooking the lake.
- D2 breakfast 06:15 [Hanoi] **Chợ Châu Long (Chau Long Market)** — Chợ Châu Long (Chau Long Market)
    - hours: Opens around 05:30, busiest 06:00–08:00.
    - price: 30,000–50,000 VND/person.
    - reason: A working neighbourhood market: live fish in basins, women shelling crab, a dozen breakfast stalls along the interior aisles.
- D2 lunch 12:00 [Hanoi] **Phở Hà** — Phở Hà
    - price: ~60,000–80,000 VND.
    - order: Order **phở gà** (chicken pho, the northern default more than beef) and **bún thang**, a clear broth with shredded egg crêpe, chicken, pork sausage, dried shrimp and a whisper of shrimp paste: the most technically demanding dish in the Hanoi repertoire.

- D7 other  [Hue] **Chợ Đông Ba (Dong Ba Market)** — Chợ Đông Ba
    - price: Go in the evening for *bánh ép*, rice-and-shrimp batter pressed flat on a hot iron and rolled with herbs and green mango, 10,000 VND a piece.
    - reason: Hue's central market; the food section on the ground floor is where the city eats.
- D8 breakfast 06:15 [Hue] **Bún bò Huế** — Bún Bò Huế Bà Tuyết, Quán Cẩm
    - reason: The dish: beef and pork bones, lemongrass, shrimp paste, chilli oil, thick round rice noodles, brisket and pork knuckle.
- D8 lunch 12:45 [Hue] **on the tomb loop** — (no named place)
    - reason: Ask the driver; there are simple places near the tombs.
- D8 dinner 19:00 [Hue] **whatever you didn't get to last night** — (no named place)
- D9 breakfast 06:30 [Hue] **Hue, before the bikes** — Cơm Hến Bà Đen, Cơm hến Hương
    - price: ~30,000 VND.
    - reason: Hue's breakfast, and the dish you will most regret not eating.
- D9 lunch 11:00 [Hoi An] **Lang Co** — Lang Co
    - price: 150,000–250,000 VND/person.
    - reason: Seafood at a beach *quán*: grilled squid, clams, oysters.
- D9 other  [Hoi An] **Mì Quảng Bà Mua** — Mì Quảng Bà Mua
    - price: Several branches; 95A Nguyen Tri Phuong (Hai Chau) and 19 Tran Binh Trong are the most often named, ~06:30–22:00 depending on branch, **35,000–50,000 VND**.
    - reason: Thick turmeric rice noodles with barely any broth, shrimp and pork, peanuts, herbs, and a rice cracker snapped over the top.
- D9 dinner 19:00 [Hoi An] **Hoi An: cao lầu** — (no named place)
    - order: Order it alongside *mì Quảng* at any busy local place in the old town; ask the hotel which this year.
    - reason: Thick, chewy noodles with char siu, greens and crackling, made with water from a specific Hoi An well; it genuinely only exists here.
