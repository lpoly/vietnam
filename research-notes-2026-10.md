# Research notes: October 2026 update

Web research done 17–19 October 2026 for the restructured route. Every new or changed number in the itinerary traces back to a line here. "VT" means vietnamtourism.com, a private guide site, not the government tourism site. Where sources disagree, all values are listed. These notes are not shown on the site.

## Hanoi
- **Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum**
  - Closed Mondays and Fridays. Annual maintenance closure runs 4 Sep–2 Nov 2026, so it is **open Sunday 13 Dec** (mornings).
  - Cool-season hours: Sat 08:00–11:30, Sun 08:00–11:00. Source: news.tuoitre.vn (…10326080923215558.htm), 9 Aug 2026.
  - VT gives Sat and Sun 08:00–11:30 (18 Sep 2026).
  - Free entry. Rules: shoulders and knees covered, no shorts, bags stored, no phones or photos, silence, hats off.
- **Stilt House / Presidential Palace grounds**
  - Price: 25,000 VND (VT; mia.vn) or 40,000 VND (blogs).
  - Winter hours: 08:00–11:00 and 13:30–16:30 (mia.vn), or 08:00–11:00 only (vnexpress). Closed Monday afternoon.
- **Temple of Literature**
  - 70,000 VND (VT, 18 Sep 2026; one 2026 blog says 30,000 VND).
  - 08:00–17:00, last entry 16:30, cash only.
  - Night tour: 199,000 VND, "typically Thu–Sun" 19:00–22:00, book 2+ days ahead (vanmieunighttour.com). December dates not found.
- **Hoa Lo Prison**
  - 08:00–17:00 daily (hoalo.vn).
  - Ticket 50,000 VND per junglebosstours (2026). Other sources: 40,000 (thuvienphapluat, May 2025) or 30,000 (older).
  - "Sacred Night" tour: Fri and Sat 19:00–21:00, 399,000–499,000 VND, ages 10–75, book ahead.
- **Museum of Ethnology** — 40,000 VND, 08:30–17:30, closed Mondays (vme-en.vass.gov.vn, official).
- **Ngoc Son Temple** — 30,000 VND, about 07:00–18:00 (VT, 5 Oct 2026).
- **Walking street** — Fri 19:00 to Sun 24:00; suspended in bad weather.
- **Thang Long Imperial Citadel** — 100,000 VND since 1 Jan 2025, 08:00–17:00 (hoangthanhthanglong.vn, official).
- **Thang Long Water Puppet Theatre** (57B Dinh Tien Hoang)
  - Tickets 100,000 / 150,000 / 200,000 VND.
  - Shows Mon–Sat 16:10, 17:20, 18:30, 20:00; Sunday adds 09:30, 11:00, 14:00 (VT, 18 Sep 2026). Other sources list only 16:10, 17:20, 18:30.
  - Book 3–7 days ahead. **thanglongwaterpuppet.org is not the theatre's site** (hijacked domain).
- **Long Bien Bridge** — fewer trains since 27 Feb 2026 (Vietcetera, 10 Mar 2026).
- **Train Street** — tour groups banned and barriers in place; cafés still operating under police watch at train times (Tuoi Tre, 11 Aug 2026).
- **Tran Quoc Pagoda** — free, about 07:30–11:30 and 13:30–18:00 (VT, 6 Oct 2026).
- **Hotels** (Kayak snapshots, 17 Oct 2026)
  - La Siesta Classic Ma May (94 Ma May): 9.5 rating, from $47, weekend average $152.
  - La Siesta Premium Hang Be (27 Hang Be): 9.5 rating, from $49, weekend average $83.
  - Hanoi Emerald Waters (47 Lo Su): 9.5 rating, weekend average $88.
  - Essence Hanoi (22 Ta Hien) now appears as "Aira Boutique"; no rate found.

## Ninh Binh
- **Trang An** — 300,000 VND adult since 1 Jan 2026, was 250,000 (baovanhoa.vn, 31 Mar 2026).
  - Same price on all routes; includes a seat in a 4–5 person boat.
  - Oct–Dec hours 07:00–16:30.
  - Route 3 suspended 31 Jul 2026 for high water; Routes 1 and 2 kept running.
- **Mua Cave** — 150,000 VND since 1 Apr 2026, was 100,000 (baovanhoa.vn). Open 06:00–19:00.
- **Hoa Lu ancient capital** — 20,000 VND (dulichninhbinh.com.vn, Mar 2025; vietnamvacation, Jan 2026).
- **Van Long**
  - Entry 20,000 VND plus boat 60,000 VND (vietnamvacation, Jan 2026), or boat 50,000 (vinwonders).
  - Alternatively 100,000 VND per person (provincial list, Mar 2025).
- **Bich Dong** — free.
- **Tam Coc boat** — 250,000 VND (dulichninhbinh, Mar 2025).
- **Thung Nham** — 250,000 VND since 1 Apr 2026.
- **Bai Dinh** — the largest pagoda complex in Vietnam.
- **Tam Coc Garden** — a boutique resort, not a homestay. From ~US$248 per night for 2 adults (Expedia, May 2026). Late check-out "for a fee, subject to availability".
- **Tam Coc Rice Fields Resort** — from €39 (Expedia, Jul 2026).
- **Day-use rates** — none found anywhere. Book a second night, or late check-out in writing.

## Hue
- **Imperial City**
  - 200,000 VND adult (official price list effective 1 Jan 2023, still applied in 2026).
  - Winter (16 Oct–15 Mar) hours: 07:00–17:30, ticket sales until 17:00 (sovaba.travel, Jul 2026). Others: 07:00–17:00 (tnktravel), 06:30–17:30 (hoianprivatetaxi, Jan 2026).
  - No regular night opening (one-off holiday events only). Allow 2–3 h.
- **Tombs**
  - Tu Duc, Khai Dinh and Minh Mang: 150,000 VND each.
  - Combos: Imperial City + 3 tombs, 530,000 VND, valid 2 days. Imperial City + 2 tombs, 420,000.
  - "All sites" combo: 580,000 (sovaba, Aug 2026) or 600,000.
  - Hours 07:00–17:30.
  - Distances from town: Tu Duc ~7 km, Khai Dinh ~10 km, Minh Mang ~12 km.
  - Time on site: Khai Dinh ~40–45 min, Minh Mang ~50 min.
- **Tomb day by 16-seat van with driver** — US$50 (4 sites) to $65 (5 sites), Hue Private Cars. General full-day car 800,000–1,200,000 VND.
- **Thien Mu Pagoda**
  - Free, 08:00–18:00, ~4 km upriver of the Citadel, 30–45 min.
  - Dragon boat: ~250,000 VND (daytripsvietnam) or from 400,000 VND (bestpricetravel). Sunset trips from 5 Le Loi around 16:00.
- **Hotels** (snapshots)
  - White Lotus Hue (5–7 Hoang Hoa Tham): 9.2 rating, ~US$68 with breakfast.
  - Alba Spa Hotel (29 Tran Quang Khai): ~$28–30.
  - Moonlight Hotel (20 Pham Ngu Lao).
  - Silk Path Grand (2 Le Loi): $59–110.
- **December weather** — average high 23.8 °C, low 18.6 °C; ~350 mm over ~20 rain days. Flood peak is Oct–Nov; no December flood found in 2024 or 2025.

## Last day: Hai Van → Hoi An, and flights
- **Hoi An Ancient Town**
  - 120,000 VND for foreigners (danangfantasticity, Jan and Mar 2026). Sites open 08:00–17:00.
  - Ticket models vary by source: 5 sites from ~22, valid 10 days (older), or two tiers of 80,000 / 120,000 VND, valid up to 3 days (2026).
  - Winter pedestrian zone 09:00–21:00.
  - Night market (Nguyen Hoang) about 17:00–22:00/23:00.
  - Lantern boat: 170,000 VND for 1–3 people, 220,000 for 4–5, about 16:00–21:30 (trvlogue, Sep 2026).
  - Japanese Covered Bridge reopened 3 Aug 2024.
- **Hoi An floods** — risk is highest late Oct to mid-Dec. On 15 Dec 2016 the old-town streets were under 1.5–2 m of water. Record floods in Oct–Nov 2025.
- **Hoi An → DAD**
  - ~30 km: 40–50 min, up to 90 min in heavy rain.
  - Grab is scarce at night; book the evening before.
  - Private car 270,000–500,000 VND; 16-seat van 450,000–750,000 VND.
- **One-way bikes Hue → Hoi An**
  - Hue Scooter Rental: from US$25, luggage 50,000 VND per piece. Route ~150 km via Lang Co, Hai Van and Marble Mountain; drop-off at 200 Le Thanh Tong, Hoi An.
  - Motorvina: from US$36 including bag transfer.
  - Guided rides take 7–9 h with stops.
- **Marble Mountains** — 40,000 VND to climb, 80,000 VND by lift (huescooterrental listing; wording ambiguous).
- **Hoi An hotels** (Kayak "from" prices, Oct 2026): Emm Hotel $76, River Suites $66, Hoi An Historic $99, Lantana $105, Little Hoi An $129.
- **My Son** — 150,000 VND, 06:00–18:00 (or 17:00), 37–50 km from Hoi An, half a day.
- **Heritage train Hue → Da Nang**
  - HĐ1 07:05 → 10:10; HĐ3 14:30 → 18:15 (vietnamnet, Aug 2026). The 2024 launch times were 07:45 / 14:25.
  - 180,000–500,000 VND; relaunched Mar 2026.
  - Suspended during the Oct 2025 floods. No 2026 confirmation that it runs on Sundays in December.
